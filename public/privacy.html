1. Who we are and our role

UltraModern CRM provides software that businesses (“customers”) use to manage leads, customers, deals, marketing campaigns, advertising, messaging, calls and staff.

  • When a business stores data in the CRM about its own leads, customers or employees, that business decides what is collected and why. We process that data on the business's behalf and only to provide the service. If you are a lead or customer of a business that uses UltraModern CRM, please contact that business first. You can also contact us and we will pass your request on.
  • For account data of people who sign up and use the CRM, such as names, work emails and login activity, we decide how it is used, as described here.

2. Information we collect

Account and user information

  • Company name, user names, work email addresses, roles and permissions.
  • Passwords. These are stored only as one-way hashes, never in readable form.
  • Login sessions, the device identifier of a desktop installation used for licence activation, and settings.

Business data that customers add

  • Leads and contacts: name, phone number, email address, organisation, city, budget, source, notes, tags, lead score and custom form answers.
  • Deals, quotations, payments, tasks, campaigns and workflow history.
  • Messages and calls: WhatsApp, SMS and email messages sent or received through the CRM, delivery status, opt-out (STOP) requests, IVR call details and the text of what callers say to the voice assistant.
  • Human resources data, if the HR module is used: employee profiles, attendance, leave and payroll records.

Advertising data from connected accounts

  • Ad account, campaign, ad set and ad names, statuses, budgets and performance figures such as impressions, clicks, spend and leads.
  • Lead form submissions from Facebook and Instagram lead ads: the details a person enters in the form, such as name, phone number, email address and answers to the form's questions.

Technical information

  • IP address, browser and device type, request logs and error logs used to keep the service secure and working.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the CRM: storing records, showing dashboards and reports, and running the automations and workflows a customer sets up.
  • To send the messages, campaigns and calls a customer asks the CRM to send, and to record replies.
  • To import leads from connected ad accounts and to show campaign performance.
  • To apply campaign changes, such as pausing a campaign or changing its budget, when an authorised user of the customer requests or approves them.
  • To provide AI features (see section 6).
  • To secure the service, prevent abuse, investigate errors and provide support.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use customer data, including data received from Meta or Google, for our own advertising or to build advertising profiles.

4. Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp data

When a customer connects a Facebook or Instagram business account, we request only the permissions needed for the features they use:

  • ads_read, ads_management, business_management: to list the customer's ad accounts, show campaign performance, and pause, resume or change campaign budgets when the customer asks.
  • leads_retrieval, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_ads: to import lead form submissions from the customer's Pages into their CRM.
  • WhatsApp Business Platform (whatsapp_business_messaging, whatsapp_business_management): to send and receive WhatsApp messages and templates for the customer's business number.

Data received from Meta is used only to provide these features to the customer who connected the account. It is not shared with other customers or sold. Access tokens are stored encrypted. A customer can disconnect at any time in the CRM, or remove the app in Facebook under Settings › Business Integrations. Once disconnected, we stop collecting new data from that account.

5. Google user data

When a customer chooses “Sign in with Google” to connect Google Ads, we request the Google Ads scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords). We use it only to read the customer's campaigns and performance figures and to apply the status or budget changes the customer requests or approves.

UltraModern CRM's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not use Google user data to serve advertisements, and we do not sell it or use it to train AI models. People do not read it except with the customer's permission, for security or abuse investigations, or where the law requires.

6. AI features

The CRM includes optional AI features: lead scoring, suggested replies, message drafting, sentiment and call summaries, natural-language search, an automated chat and voice assistant, and advertising recommendations. To produce a result, the relevant text, such as a customer message or a lead's details, is sent to the AI provider the CRM is configured to use. Currently that is Groq; OpenAI or Google Gemini may be used instead. The provider returns the result to the CRM.

We send only the data needed for the task. We do not use customer data to train our own or anyone else's AI models. AI recommendations that would change ad spending wait for approval by an authorised user.

7. Who we share information with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the CRM, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Cloud hosting and database providers (for example MongoDB Atlas).
  • Messaging and calling providers that deliver the customer's messages and calls (for example the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, Twilio, and SMS gateways the customer chooses).
  • Email delivery providers.
  • AI providers, as described in section 6.
  • Meta and Google, when a customer connects their accounts and asks the CRM to act on them.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of users or the public, or to a successor if the business is reorganised or sold. In a sale, this policy will continue to apply to the data.

8. How we protect information

  • Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Passwords are hashed. Access tokens and messaging-provider credentials are encrypted when stored and are never shown in full in the app.
  • Each customer's data is kept separate from every other customer's data.
  • Role-based permissions limit what each user can see and change, and important actions are recorded in an audit log.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects personal data, we will notify affected customers and authorities as the law requires.

9. How long we keep information

  • Business data is kept while the customer's account is active, until the customer deletes it.
  • When an account is closed, or a valid deletion request is received, we delete the data within 30 days. We keep it longer only where the law requires.
  • Error logs are deleted automatically 30 days after the last occurrence.
  • Data from Meta or Google is deleted when the customer disconnects the account and asks us to remove it, or when the customer's account is deleted.

10. Data deletion requests

You can ask us to delete your personal data at any time:

  • CRM users and customers: delete individual records inside the CRM, or email us to close your account and delete all of its data.
  • People whose details were collected by a business using our CRM, for example through a Facebook or Instagram lead form or a WhatsApp chat: email us with the phone number or email address you used. We will delete your data or pass the request to that business, and confirm when it is done.
  • Facebook users: you can also remove the app under Facebook Settings › Business Integrations and then email us to delete the data received from it.

Send requests to ultramoderntechnologies@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. We will confirm within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • have your data deleted;
  • withdraw consent;
  • raise a grievance with us, and with the relevant authority if it is not resolved.

You can stop SMS and WhatsApp marketing messages at any time by replying STOP. To use any other right, email us at the address below.

12. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The CRM uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your settings. Signing out clears it.

13. Children

UltraModern CRM is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

14. Where data is processed

Our service providers may store or process data outside your country, including outside India. When they do, we use providers that protect it with appropriate security and contractual safeguards.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. We will tell customers about significant changes by email or inside the app before they take effect.
